**Written:** 2026-11-04 evening by Claude Code (Track A), as a read-and-document audit. No code or config was changed for this report.

**Scope:** `main` at `e98d919`, plus the Track B and Track C worktrees.

**How to read the evidence tags:**
- **[V]** = verified in this audit, in code, the database, git, cron or the file system.
- **[D]** = stated in a doc or a track report and not re-checked here.
- **[P]** = from Prajna's own records (Prajna is the older system), not re-verified here.

All research numbers are **survivorship-biased and EXPLORATORY**, as the research docs say. Nothing in Drishti is a validated edge or a prediction.

## 1. Executive summary

Drishti set out to be an agentic, ML-driven NSE trading system. Swing trading came first; intraday was planned next. The system would:
- test strategies on history;
- pick stocks daily;
- read charts and candles live;
- predict up/down with ML;
- learn from reward and penalty;
- record every prediction;
- paper-trade.

**What exists today [V]:**
- a solid, point-in-time data stack (Upstox ingest, a tick recorder, corporate-action adjustment, continuity checks against Prajna);
- a research and replay stack with honest controls (a seeded random-entry placebo, purged and embargoed walk-forward CV, a deflated Sharpe ratio, a trial ledger);
- a daily watchlist (Scout);
- a decision-support scanner, a paper journal and a Live Radar;
- a historical odds engine (the "Time Machine");
- a chart UI and Telegram delivery.

**The single most important finding:** no entry signal tested so far beat a same-stock random-entry placebo after costs. Here are the numbers:

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| v1 rules vs placebo | −0.100 R vs −0.090 R |
| ML model #1 | AUC 0.505 |
| Only sprint finalist, PEAD 60-session (C3b) | failed its holdout by −0.202 R |
| Hammer/candle family (13 variants) | closed |
| Momentum book (C1a) | after tax ties/loses to a Nifty200 Momentum 30 index fund; excess-return DSR 0.23–0.27 at N≈30 |

So the project was **reframed** from a prediction and auto-trading engine into a **decision-support system**. It is a scanner with honest levels and costs, a journal that compares your own trades with the alerts and with a placebo, and an odds engine that tells you what a stop/target geometry historically did on that stock. There is **no ML edge, no forward test running, no automated paper trader and no order placement**. The user decides every trade, on paper.

## 2. Original vision and goals

Sources: `docs/DESIGN.md` (plan of 2026-10-28, approved with 8 changes), `docs/SPEC_drishti-swing-v1.md` (frozen at P0, tag `p0-freeze`), `docs/PREREG_M1.md` (text frozen 2026-10-28) and `docs/TRACKS.md`.

**Goals:**
- **Test strategies on history:** a daily replay/backtest from 2019 to the cut-off, with costs, scored against a placebo and rules-only.
- **Pick stocks daily:** Scout (Screener B) at 19:30, 100–300 names, as context for the chart reader.
- **Read charts and candles:** 26 candle flags, S/R zones, trend, breakout/retest state.
- **Strategies:** S1a/S1b (momentum breakout and retest), S2 (EMA20 pullback), S3 (support + reversal candle). S4/S5 (ORB, VWAP) were planned for intraday model #2.
- **Predict up/down with ML:**
  - model #1 (swing, daily bars) as LightGBM + isotonic calibration on triple-barrier labels (target capped at min(2R, resistance), −1R stop, 5-session time-out);
  - purged and embargoed walk-forward CV;
  - model #2 (15m intraday) after model #1's forward test starts.
- **Reward/penalty learning:** nightly grading of every prediction, plus a weekly challenger model in shadow while the champion stays frozen until its look.
- **Record every prediction:** append-only tables (`prediction`, `grade`, `paper_order`, `paper_fill`, …).
- **Paper trading:**
  - a Risk Manager (1 % risk, ≤ 3 % open risk, ≤ 2 per sector);
  - a Paper Trader with T+1 daily-bar fills;
  - a Narrator (an LLM news veto);
  - Telegram.
- **Forward test (PREREG_M1):** at most 3 hypotheses (net expectancy > 0, beats rules-only, Brier skill > 0). One look, after ≥ 40 sessions and ≥ 100 closed signal events, using a bootstrap CI with Bonferroni.
- **"Agentic":** Scout → Chart Reader → Strategist → Predictor → Risk Manager → Paper Trader → Judge, run as a nightly pipeline. A Redis bus was planned for intraday.

**Hard rules [V]** (`CLAUDE.md`):
- PAPER ONLY;
- never print secrets;
- Upstox data only (no NSE scraping);
- back up and verify the sha256 before any DB change;
- no training or looks inside a forward window.

### 3.3 Scanner and alerts (decision support)

- **Code:** `scanner/detect.py` (`scan-v1`), `scanner/run.py`, `scanner/grade.py`; migration 0008. [V]
- **Detectors:** `HAMMER_CONFIRM`, `BREAKOUT_52W`, `SUPPORT_REVERSAL` (S3-style), `EMA20_PULLBACK` (S2-style). They reuse `drishti.features`; no new entry research went into them.
- **Levels:**
  - entry = a buy-stop 0.05 % above the bar's high;
  - stop = the wider of (structure low − 0.1 ATR) and (entry − 1 ATR);
  - R1/R2 = the next resistance zones;
  - qty at 1 % of ₹10 lakh, and the round-trip cost in R.
- **Actionable target (2026-11-04, `97fd80b`) [V]:**
  - The net R:R to R1 is computed as the odds engine does it: costs included, and a stop booked at −1.2 R.
  - If it is < 1, the target is R2 (if its net R:R ≥ 1), else entry + 2R.
  - Tags: "broken R1" (a hit at R1 is ≤ 0 net R) or "low R:R". R1/R2 stay as reference levels.
  - It is computed from stored fields, so old alerts get it too.
  - Of the 24 EOD alerts, 8 are flagged (for example JINDALPOLY: R1 is 0.20 above the entry, so the target is now R2).
- **Jobs [V]:**
  - `scan_eod` 19:45 on the eligible top 500. It also scores the session's odds and sends the Telegram messages.
  - `scan_intraday` every 15 min on the watchlist plus the Live Radar names, using the last closed 15m bar.
  - `scan_grade` 09:45.
- **Point in time [V]** (`5427b1c`):
  - Detection sees only bars up to the alert bar; pivots are confirmed 5 bars later.
  - A re-run for a past day uses `as_of` = 19:45 of that day.
  - Each alert stores the R1/R2 zones it used, with pivot dates, plus `as_of`.
  - The 11-02 batch was a one-off re-run with the official bar. One of its 14 alerts (GABRIEL) would not have fired live, so the stats count from 11-03.
- **Grading [V]:**
  - triple barrier with the frozen `exit_path` (stop first, gap rules);
  - three bases: `alert`, `took` (the user's entry) and `placebo` (a seeded random entry in the next bar);
  - **15m alerts are flat by the end of their session** (`grade.slots_left`, 2026-11-04);
  - 1d alerts run 5 sessions on official bars;
  - live 15m grades (`journal_outcome.source = 'live'`) are not replaced by the nightly grader.
- **Counts today [V]:**
  - 135 alerts: 24 × 1d (11-02, 11-03) and 111 × 15m (11-04).
  - Latest `alert` grade, 15m: target 9 · stop 12 · expired 29 · not triggered 61.
  - 1d: 20 OPEN, 4 not triggered.
  - **These samples are far too small to mean anything.**

### 3.4 Live Radar (intraday re-scan)

- **Code:** `scanner/radar.py` (`radar-v1`), migration 0009, `GET /api/radar`; commit `fba23c1`. [V]
- **How it works:** every 15 min it applies Scout's criteria (PARAMS unchanged) to the liquid/F&O universe on a **partial daily bar**:
  - The partial bar uses the recorder's closed 1m bars plus the day OHLC at the first tick seen before the bar closed.
  - History is the adjusted official 1d up to T−1.
  - The universe is fixed for the day from T−1. On 11-04 it was 500 names; all 213 eligible F&O names were already inside the top 500.
- **New qualifiers** (not on the EOD watchlist, not yet on today's radar) get one `radar_event` row with the criteria that flipped since T−1. The 15m scan then watches them.
- **Tests:** the parity of `radar.criteria` with Scout's `screen()` on the same bars is tested.
- **Status:** **0 rows so far.** The recorder only subscribes the radar universe from 11-05, so **11-05 is the first full day**. A dry run on 11-04 (rolled back, nothing stored) found that the 14 names passing were all already on the watchlist.

### 3.6 Time Machine / odds engine

- **Engine:** `judge/odds.py` (Track B, `odds-1d-v1`, merged at `d99137d`). [V]
  - a stationary block-bootstrap Monte Carlo (10k paths), plus a historical replay of the same %-geometry;
  - the same barrier rule (stop first, gap-through at the open), stop exits ≥ −1.2 R, net of `core.costs`;
  - the verdict comes from the net break-even 1/(1+R_net);
  - **RESEARCH / SIMULATION, NOT a signal.**
- **Swing (live) [V]:**
  - `scanner/odds_job.py` scores the session's 1d alerts. It runs inside `scan_eod`; the 20:15 `odds` cron is a backstop and is **not installed**.
  - Results go to `odds_result` (migration 0010) and the view `odds_latest` (0011, with flat outputs and `thin_sample` when replay N < 30).
  - **Point in time:** data as knowable at the alert's 19:45 scan, so it is reproducible.
  - Horizon = `grade.HOLD["1d"]` = 5. The cost-model label is recorded.
  - Idempotent: an unchanged plan is skipped.
  - Current contents: 48 rows across 24 alerts.
  - Typical verdicts are "MOSTLY NEITHER" (most paths hit neither barrier in 5 sessions) and "BASE RATES LOSE".
- **15m: pending.**
  - The API returns "odds pending: 15m engine" for 15m alerts. The daily engine must not score 15m trades.
  - Track B has an **uncommitted** `judge/odds15.py` (439 lines, "INTERIM", Prajna 15m history) in its worktree [V exists; content not reviewed].
  - Track B's `9374b73` (odds v2: explicit horizon per plan, replay N, THIN SAMPLE flag) is **committed on `track-b` and not merged**.
- **Calibration:** `odds.calibration()` compares predicted target-first rates with realised grades. It needs about **100 graded plans** to be meaningful; today **0** 1d alerts have a final outcome.

## 4. Research rigor and key findings

**Methodology [V code]:**
- Replays on official adjusted bars with the spec's costs (10 bps/side base; 5 and 20 bps sensitivities).
- A **seeded random-entry placebo on the same stock with the same exits and holding period** for every rule result.
- Purged and embargoed walk-forward CV for the ML (`ml/cv_purged.py`).
- A development window up to 2024-12-31 and a holdout from 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-21, **already used once** (by C3b and by the C1a override).
- The deflated Sharpe ratio (`judge/dsr.py`).
- The trial ledger (`research/track-b/sprint/TRIAL_LEDGER.md`).

The trial ledger currently counts **39 rule sets (42 with the model grid), so N ≈ 40**. Any future candidate must clear DSR + SPA at that N against a same-exit placebo before any forward test.

**Results** (all [D] from DESIGN.md / the ledger / the research files; numbers in R are net):

| Family | Result |
|---|---|
| **v1 rules S1a/S1b/S2/S3** (set b, 2020-09 → 2026-10-21, 2,381 closed) | rules **−0.100 R** vs placebo **−0.090 R**. By strategy: S1a −0.046, S1b −0.326, S2 −0.101, S3 −0.114. Negative at 5 and 20 bps and in every year except 2020 |
| v1 close-confirm entry | −0.176 R |
| **ML model #1** (LightGBM + isotonic, 22 folds) | (b) AUC **0.505**, BSS +0.003; (a) AUC 0.494. No threshold gives a positive mean net R |
| v1 exit variants V01–V05 | rules at or below placebo in every one (for example V04 −0.021 vs +0.009) |
| C2a/C2b (S1a + trailing SMA) | below placebo (−0.050 vs +0.167; +0.324 vs +0.465) |
| C3a PEAD 20-session | +0.012 over placebo (dev) |
| **C3b PEAD 60-session** (the only finalist) | dev +0.113 over placebo; **holdout −0.202 R** (+0.065 vs +0.267). Failed |
| **Momentum book C1a** | holdout +1.6 pp vs the Momentum 30 *price index* pre-tax (supporting only: a reused window). **After tax it ties with or loses to a Momentum 30 index fund** (holdout CAGR −5.1 % vs −6.2 %, deeper drawdown −20.3 % vs −15.9 %). **Excess-return DSR 0.23–0.27 at N ≈ 30.** CLOSED 2026-11-03, no forward test |
| **Hammer / candle family** RJ1–RJ9, SW-a…d | all fail vs same-exit placebos (best SW-c +0.08 R on 38 trades, not significant). CLOSED 2026-11-02 |
| Watchlist buy setups (12, 2026-11-03) | none beats the all-bars baseline at T+5 (best +0.20 %, t ≈ 1); selection-biased sample |
| **ORB / VWAP** | **not tested in Drishti.** S4/S5 belong to the intraday model #2 (P7), which was never started. Prajna runs a P1 shadow forward test of ORB/VWAP from 2026-10-27; it is not looked at and stops at the switch [P] |
| Prajna history [P] | E5 ML ensemble: out-of-time look NOT_ACCEPTED (2026-10-26). E4 gap fade: ACCEPTED-EXPLORATORY but slippage-fragile. E4b stopped |

**The central result, stated plainly:** across about 40 rule sets and an ML model, **no entry signal beat a same-stock random entry with the same exits after costs.** Where returns were positive, the placebo was positive too: market drift and exit geometry, not entry skill. The one candidate that passed development (C3b) failed its holdout. The only "pass" (C1a) did not survive tax and deflation.

**Consequence:**
- v1 is frozen and **not** taken forward; P6 and the forward test are ON HOLD. No v2 exists.
- The system was **reframed into decision support**:
  - a scanner whose alerts are explicitly "not validated edges";
  - a journal that grades the user's own trades against the alerts and a placebo;
  - an odds engine that reports what a geometry historically did, with its own label "a reality check, … not a signal".

## 8. Risks and caveats

- **No validated edge.** Every detector, setup, radar name and odds verdict is decision support. The samples (24 swing alerts, 111 15m alerts, 0 journal trades) say nothing about skill. With N ≈ 40 trials already spent, a chance "winner" is likely, and so is believing it.
- **Overtrading risk.** About 100 15m alerts a day with no edge invite action. This is why the routing sends 15m alerts as digests and every message says "not a prediction … Your call".
- **15m bars are unadjusted (D5).** For a stock with a split or bonus in the last ~25 days, the intraday zones, R1/R2, stop and indicators can be wrong. **The 11-20 switch alone does not fix this**; the fix is part of the re-point.
- **Odds-engine stationarity.** The odds come from the stock's own last ~3 years. Results, news, circuits and regime changes break that. Thin replay samples are flagged (N < 30). It is a reality check on geometry, not a forecast.
- **Survivorship bias.** Prajna's history was backfilled from the current master, so names delisted in 2019–2025 are mostly missing. Every backtest number is biased upward (`DESIGN.md`).
- **Data sources:**
  - Upstox only for stored data. The vendor serves only ~1 year of CA history (88 events exist only in Prajna and the archive), drops older news of busy stocks, and fills T−1 intraday slowly overnight.
  - Yahoo is not used by Drishti at all (Prajna had a research-only Yahoo collector) [V `PRAJNA_INVENTORY.md`].
  - There is no NSE fetching.
- **Ops risks:**
  - a shared token and Prajna dependence until the switch;
  - no full Prajna backup until the switch night (D3);
  - WebSocket reconnects (keepalive timeouts; loop gap < 1 s, so on the vendor/network side);
  - the larger tick volume from 11-05;
  - uncommitted edits by other sessions have appeared in `main`'s tree twice (11-03, 11-04).
- **Telegram:** the same bot/chat as V1. If V1 is ever restarted it will post there too.
